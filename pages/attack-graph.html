How to read this graph

  • Shape identifies the entity type; colour follows the legend below the graph.
  • The dashed outer ring marks risk level: critical, high, medium or low.
  • A soft glow marks entities that behaved suspiciously in the sample records.
  • Edge labels appear on hover, on selection, and along the highlighted path.
Layout coordinates are hand-authored and identical on every load — the graph does not simulate physics.
